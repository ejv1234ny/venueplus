# VenuePlus Service Provider Agreement

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This agreement supplements the Terms of Service for service providers.

## 1. Independent Contractor
You are an independent contractor and **not** an employee, agent, or partner of VenuePlus. You are responsible for your own tools, equipment, transportation, taxes, insurance, and any required licenses or certifications.

## 2. Listings & Availability
- Maintain accurate service description, hourly rate, service area, and availability.
- You may set blackout dates.
- You may decline non-mandatory job offers without penalty. Repeated declines or no-shows may impact ranking.

## 3. Job Offers & Acceptance
- When you accept a job offer, you commit to performing the service at the agreed time and place.
- Once accepted, you must notify the host and renter via the in-app messaging system if anything changes.

## 4. Quality Standards
- You will perform services in a professional manner consistent with industry standards.
- You will follow venue rules.
- For categories that require licensing (security, food service, alcohol service), you will maintain valid credentials.

## 5. Payouts
- VenuePlus charges a platform fee (currently 12% of service subtotal).
- Payouts are issued via Stripe Connect within 24 hours of event completion.
- You are responsible for your own taxes; Stripe will issue 1099-K forms where required.

## 6. Insurance
You should carry commercial general liability insurance appropriate to your category. Some venues may require proof of insurance before accepting your job.

## 7. Background Checks
For certain categories (security, staff, in-home services), VenuePlus may require periodic background checks via a third-party provider. Refusal disqualifies you from those categories.

## 8. Anti-Circumvention
You agree not to solicit bookings from VenuePlus customers off-platform after meeting through the Service.

## 9. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time. Open job commitments must be honored or properly cancelled.
