# VenuePlus Host Agreement

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Host Agreement supplements the VenuePlus Terms of Service for users who list venues.

## 1. Eligibility
You must own or have legal authority to rent the venue you list. You are responsible for any HOA, lease, or municipal restrictions.

## 2. Listings
- You must accurately describe the venue, capacity, amenities, photos, and rules.
- You set your own price, minimum hours, and cancellation policy.
- You may require mandatory services (cleaning, security, etc.). The cost will be added to renter checkout automatically.

## 3. Acceptance & Conduct
- You commit to responding to booking requests within a reasonable time.
- You must honor confirmed bookings except for documented emergencies.
- Repeated cancellations may result in account suspension.

## 4. Payouts
- VenuePlus charges a platform fee (currently 12% of venue subtotal). Fees may change with notice.
- Payouts are issued via Stripe Connect within 24 hours of event completion.
- You are responsible for your own taxes. Stripe will issue 1099-K forms where required.

## 5. Insurance
You should maintain commercial general liability insurance covering events at your venue. Renters may also be required to provide event insurance.

## 6. Damage & Disputes
- Damage claims must be opened within 72 hours of event end with photo evidence.
- VenuePlus may withhold damage deposits while investigating.
- We will mediate but not adjudicate disputes.

## 7. Compliance
You are responsible for compliance with local zoning, occupancy, noise, fire, and short-term rental regulations.

## 8. Termination
You may delist at any time. Confirmed future bookings must still be honored unless cancelled per policy.
