# VenuePlus Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account info:** name, email, phone, password (hashed)
- **Profile info:** bio, photos, business details (for hosts/providers)
- **Listings:** venue/service descriptions, addresses, photos, pricing
- **Bookings & messages:** transaction history, in-app conversations
- **Payment info:** handled by Stripe; we store only Stripe customer IDs and tokenized references
- **Verification:** ID documents, business licenses, insurance certificates (when required)
- **Usage data:** IP, device, browser, pages visited, clicks
- **Location data:** approximate location for search

## 2. How We Use It
- Provide and operate the Service
- Match renters with venues and service providers
- Process payments and payouts
- Send transactional and marketing communications
- Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Sharing
- **With other users**, only what is needed to complete a transaction (e.g. host sees renter name and event details after a confirmed booking).
- **With service providers**, payment processors (Stripe), email/SMS providers (Resend, Twilio), analytics (PostHog), error monitoring (Sentry), cloud hosting (Railway, Vercel, AWS).
- **For legal reasons**, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- **With your consent**, in any other case.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Cookies
We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and analytics cookies to improve the Service. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain your data while your account is active and for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal information
- Object to or restrict processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, email **privacy@venueplus.com**.

## 7. California (CCPA/CPRA)
California residents have additional rights including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information.

## 8. EU/UK (GDPR)
Our legal bases for processing include performance of contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests (fraud prevention, service improvement), and consent (marketing).

## 9. Children
The Service is not directed to children under 18.

## 10. Security
We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit and at rest, but no system is perfectly secure.

## 11. International Transfers
If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

## 12. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via email or in-app.
