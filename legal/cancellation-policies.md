# Cancellation Policies

VenuePlus offers three cancellation policy tiers that hosts can choose from when listing:

## Flexible
- **>7 days before event:** 100% refund
- **1–7 days before event:** 50% refund
- **<24 hours:** No refund

## Moderate
- **>14 days before event:** 100% refund
- **7–14 days before event:** 50% refund
- **<7 days:** No refund

## Strict
- **>30 days before event:** 50% refund
- **<30 days:** No refund

## Notes
- The platform fee is non-refundable in all tiers except for host-initiated cancellations.
- Host-initiated cancellations always result in a 100% refund to the renter and may incur a penalty to the host.
- Force majeure events (natural disasters, government orders) are handled case-by-case at VenuePlus discretion.

The current implementation defaults to the **Flexible** policy. Per-listing policy selection is on the roadmap.
